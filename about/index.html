The philosophy

I chose an AI & Business program over a traditional computer-science degree on purpose.

I care about how software is made — the DOM, the main thread, the data model — but I do not want that to be the whole picture. A system that is elegant in isolation and useless in a hospital, a shop, or a classroom is still a failed system. I want to understand how technology becomes a useful product that solves real problems.

That means sitting with both sides of the work: writing the interface, and asking who has to click it at 2 a.m. It means treating business constraints as part of the design, not as a layer you add later. I am early. The point of this site is to keep a public record of that learning, without inflating it.
