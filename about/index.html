02

The philosophy

Technology, in context

I am interested in the space between what technology can do and what is genuinely useful to someone.

That curiosity brings together three areas I keep returning to: artificial intelligence, software engineering, and startup building. AI can make new kinds of tools possible, but the model is only one part of the work. There is also the data, the interface, the system around it, and the question of whether it helps with a real need.

Software engineering gives me a way to explore those details. I enjoy learning how a page responds to an interaction, how information moves through a program, and how small implementation choices affect the experience. I want to understand fundamentals well enough to make things that are clear, dependable, and honest about their trade-offs.

Startup building adds another useful perspective: begin with a problem, pay attention to the people living with it, and test whether an idea holds up outside a notebook. I chose an AI & Business education because I want to study both the technology and the decisions that help it become a product. I am at the beginning of that work, and I am building in public to keep a record of what I learn along the way.
